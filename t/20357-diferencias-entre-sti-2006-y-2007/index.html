Estuve leyendo un poco sobre el auto y no sabia que habia diferencias entre estos dos años les dejo algunos, si saben de algunos mas pueden aportar.

Turbo - VF39 / VF43 (no difference in performance)
Headlight, Rear lamp housing - Grey / Chrome (lamparas traseras)
Wheel center caps - BBS / STI (centro de llanta)
Gearing - shorter 2,3,4 on 06 and previous years (cambios mas cortos del 06 para atras)
Wheelbase - 100 / 99.4 (this is gimmickry with caster, no difference)
Rear seat center pass through - no / yes
Rear diff - Helical / Torsen (I might be wrong but 07's rear diff is better)
Transmission oil pump - yes / no (07+ has scrapers instead)
