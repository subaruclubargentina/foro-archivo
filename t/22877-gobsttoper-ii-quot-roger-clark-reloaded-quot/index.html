Gobstopper 2 technical specification

Engine 2.0ltr Subaru EJ20 engine, Garett hybrid 4094 turbo, Turbosmart 50mm external wastegate, 2.0 closed deck block, RCM gas ring conversion, RCM arrow M2000 conrods,. RCM 75mm Arrow steel crankshaft, RCM lightweight Omega pistons, RCM big valve ‘O’ ring type heads, RCM engine valves, titanium retainers, RCM ‘R profile’ tight wrap cambelt system, WRC SI0 inlet manifold, RCM 8 injector kit, 1000cc injector x 8, RCM FBW throttle body, RCM 7.5 litre dry sump system, RCM Inconel short runner exhaust manifold, K&N air filter, RCM intercooler water and oil cooler system, uprated single valve springs, 4” exhaust with thermal coating, Zircotec coated manifold.

Driveline AST carbon prop, RCM custom driveshafts, RI80 STI rear axle, 3.9:1 crown wheel and pinion with custom RCM diff cover, six speed gearbox with RCM sequential mechanism incorporating Zytek paddleshift, AP Quad Plate carbon clutch, RCM low inertia 2.5kg flywheel.

Suspension/Brakes AP brakes discs - carbon 380mm (front), 340mm (rear)/steel 365mm (front), 340mm (rear), AP hydraulic handbrake, Ohlins TTX Dampers with Eibach Spring, custom RCM billet uprights, WRC arms with custom chassis mounts, RCM top mounts.

Wheels/Tyres Team Dynamics fully forged five axis milled 18×10”alloys, Pirelli slicks and wets.

Exterior Custom RCM pre-preg carbon fibre body panels, alloy STI rear hatch, lightweight windows and heated screen, honeycomb flat floor, custom aero package, Krontec Air Jacks.

Interior Recaro HANS seats/Sabelt harnesses, Lifeline safety system, AP Hydraulic handbrake, Autotel intercom, Carbon Reverie steering wheel, bespoke carbon fibre panelling, custom Fabrications T45 cage, lightweight panel work
