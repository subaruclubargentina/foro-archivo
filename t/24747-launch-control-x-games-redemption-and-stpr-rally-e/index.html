    Launch Control – Season 3, Episode 6 – “Redemption X”

    Subaru Rally Team USA is faced with an enormous task to find solutions and rebuild the Global Rallycross cars before X Games Austin. In just four days, the team has new parts and three cars – including Travis Pastrana – ready to race. With fifteen cars competing for just six spots in the finals, this event is far more cutthroat than normal.

    Meanwhile, on the same weekend in Pennsylvania, David Higgins and Craig Drew are aiming to continue their unbeaten record at the Susquehannock Trail Performance Rally, round 5 of the Rally America championship. But massive trees and no room for error mean this rally can punish any mistakes.
