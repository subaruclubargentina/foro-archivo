The Subaru Impreza WRX 22B STi is one of the rarest and most sought after examples of the Impreza built by Subaru. Officially 400 were made, only 16 were brought into the UK, although many more have found their way here via private importers.The 22B is often considered the ultimate Impreza, featuring a full WRC style wide arch body, built to celebrate the 40th anniversary of Subaru, the engine is a 2.2ltr, outputting in the region of 350bhp. The rarity of the car has led to it retaining a high proportion of its value. Subaru have yet to make another Subaru that is so close in looks to that of its rally car, although the current range does include a Spec-C, which is very close to the Group N Rally class.

DICE QUE ESTE SUBARU IMPREZA WRX 22B STI ES UNO DE LOS MAS RAROS QUE FABRICO LA SUBARU. SE HICIERON OFICIALMENTE 400 UNIDADES Y SOLO 16 ENTRARON A INGLATERRA, AUNQUE METIERON VARIOS IMPORTANDOLOS INDIVIDUALMENTE. EL 22B ES CONSIDERADO EL ULTIMO IMPREZA BIEN PARCIDO A UN WRC Y SE HIZO PARA CELEBRAR LOS 40 AÑOS DE SUBARU, EL MOTOR ES UN 2.2 LTR, DANDO 350BHP.
