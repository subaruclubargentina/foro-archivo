    Ride along with Subaru Rally Team USA's David Higgins and Craig Drew as they win stage 3 of the 2015 New England Forest Rally. With an average speed of nearly 80mph, the team jumps and slides their way to the finish over 9-seconds faster than the rest of the competition. The team went on to win the rally, securing their fifth-consecutive Rally America championship title.
