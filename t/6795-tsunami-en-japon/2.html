    Subaru of America Statement Regarding the earthquake in Japan

    FROM:
    Subaru of America, Inc.
    DEPARTMENT:
    SOA Headquarters
    DATE:
    03/11/2011
    CATEGORY:
    Show in all Categories

    Fuji Heavy Industries (FHI), manufacturer of Subaru automobiles, is conducting an assessment of all of its facilities in Japan. At this time, it is believed that no Subaru automotive production facilities have been damaged by the earthquake on Friday March 11, 2011. As it is currently the weekend in Japan, further updates will be available on Monday.

    We can confirm that no Subaru employees were injured at its Shinjuku headquarters. We are also currently assessing the situation at FHI suppliers, dealers and the impact on North American import vehicles. Production at the Subaru plant in Lafayette, Indiana continues as scheduled. Subaru produces Legacy, Outback and Tribeca vehicles for the North American market at this plant.

    At this time we do not anticipate any affect on our North American operations in the immediate future.
