"Los Angeles, California, October 12, 2010 -- Over 1200 Subaru enthusiasts from the West gathered on October 9th for the largest all-Subaru motorsport event and festival in North America: Subiefest 2010, presented by Subaru of America, Inc.

Returning for its 5th consecutive year, Subiefest took over Willow Springs (Rosamond, California) on Saturday October 9, 2010. "I think the general consensus is that this was the best Subiefest experienced offered to date," said Robert Champion, Event Director. "The racing was intense, Subaru of America's participation was epic, the car show was highly competitive, and the vendor midway was full of awesome deals and giveaways for everyone in attendance.""
