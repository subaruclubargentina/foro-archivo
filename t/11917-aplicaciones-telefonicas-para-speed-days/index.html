ROBADO DEL CUORE SPORTIVO :

Acá una listita con algunas aplicaciones para iPhone, Android y otras plataformas que pueden ser muy utiles para los días de track days. Desde cronómetros con laps hasta analizadores de fuerza G y demás chucherías....


Android

Trackmaster
"Trackmaster is a powerful racing lap timer that runs on your Android phone. Record your speed and times, analyze and replay your data, share your data on Twitter and Facebook, and export to Google Earth or MSExcel - plus much more! Works great for autocross, hillclimbs, road rally, and track days!" (EDIT: more details here)

Dynomaster
"Dynomaster is full-featured dyno and drag racing app that runs on your Android phone. It provides many functions including 1/4 mile times, power calculations, graphical analysis, G-meter, mapping, speech, import/export to Google Earth or MSExcel, sharing time slips on Twitter and Facebook - plus much more!"

Race Ace
"Race Ace provides datalogging for your car on a track or race day---lap times, course, maximum speeds, braking points and g-forces, based on data from the GPS."

Street Race
"GPS based Speedometer and timer for 1/4 mile drags and 0-60 mph sprints. Touch lights to start, wait for GPS fix and put the pedal to the metal when light hits green! Your time can be uploaded and results compared with other fast drivers."

Trackmeter
"The perfect tool for circuit regularity. Do your lap with as many as 99 references and then copy the lap to be the most regular. Ideal for regularity (TSD) in circuit or closed tracks. Allows to record the first lap to be able to copy the following laps. Also valid for trackdays/endurance."


iPhone

Track Day
"Track Day is an all-in-one tool for measuring your vehicles performance at the track. Using track day you can measure your vehicles acceleration times, record lap times automatically, and display real time cornering, acceleration and braking g-force information."

Dynolicious
"So you've already got a sweet ride... now you can measure just how sweet it is. Dynolicious uses the iPhone's built-in accelerometer to measure you vehicle's 0-60 acceleration, quarter mile elapsed time, horsepower, lateral G's, and several other performance metrics. A history of test results for your car are saved, so averages and trends can instantly be retrieved. Dynolicious also allows you to record modifications made to your car, and to evaluate the resulting gains (or losses) in power and performance."

Total Traction
"Total traction helps tune the most important performance system in your car—the driver. This is more than just another G meter. Total Traction provides continuous feedback about how close your car is to loosing traction, (skidding). It blends braking, cornering, and acceleration forces, and compares them to the car’s measured performance limits, while correcting for hills, banked corners, dips, etc., ensuring consistent and accurate results."

G Force
"G FORCE makes your iPhone or iPod Touch a handy vehicle G-Force meter. A nice looking enhancement to the dash of your car, too. Quantify the G-Force you experience in the vehicle (e.g. during acceleration, braking, drifting, cornering etc) or perform a quick dyno."

Harry's Lap Timer Pro
"Harry‘s GPS LapTimer is the iPhone tool for lap timing. Offering advanced data recording, it is the ideal companion for amateur racing. Utilizing GPS, LapTimer will automatically stop your laps and sector times. With laps stored in a database, you can track your progress while improving your lap times corner by corner. Using your existing iPhone, LapTimer is an extremely inexpensive option compared to standalone solutions."

Rev / Rev Lite
Rev monitors, displays, charts, and records real-time data by interacting with your vehicle's onboard diagnostic system. Rev can also check and reset "Check Engine" lights and trouble codes and much more.

g-tac / g-tac pro /g-tac free
Think you're quick? Think you can drive? Prove it with g-tac. g-tac is the first car performance meter app to use both accelerometer and GPS for the most accurate results.
g-tac pro uses the accelerometer and GPS in iPhone or iPod touch to track and record the performance of your vehicle at the circuit or the drag strip.

Racing Lap Timer HD
Racing Lap Timer HD is an iPad & iPhone Stopwatch application for lap time measurement.
Whether you're on the road or racing track, riding your bike or running a parcour, Laptimer HD records your: Current Lap, Average Lap, Fastest Lap, Slowest Lap and Total Time.

CMS Lap Timer Pro + Data Acquisition / Analysis
The Cantrell Motorsports Lap Timer Pro is the most advanced and easiest to use racecar GPS-based lap timer, data recorder and performance analysis app available for the iTouch.

Windows Mobile

TrakPal PitStop
"Analyse your performance using NMEA compliant GPS data recorded on your Pocket PC - the perfect complement to your GPS navigation software. Compare data from different runs to find where those important seconds were lost, whether on the track, training, commuting or just for fun. Zoom in and scroll graphs for speed, acceleration ? both laterally and forward/back ? and altitude. Graphs can be displayed either as raw recoded format or against distance. "

GPS Performance
"GPS Performance is a must have application for people with performance cars interested in performance statistics. Using a regular GPS receiver and sophisticated alorythms, GPS Performance is able to accurately measure 1/4 mile dashes and 0..60 timings. Circuits can be defined for timing laps and up to 5 split times per track can be added. Of course the runs are fully configurable, allowing custom distances and speed ranges to be specified, and tracks are created simply by driving around the circuit."
