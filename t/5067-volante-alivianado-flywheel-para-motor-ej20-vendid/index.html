La respuesta a la famosa pregunta..............

What does a flywheel do? Why buy a flywheel?

A flywheel is a component of your clutch assembly, and can make a huge difference in how your car responds when you press the gas pedal. Lightened flywheels are made out of aluminum or chromoly, which reduces the rotating mass by dramatically trimming weight as compared to your heavy factory OEM flywheel. Because the lighter flywheel has less inertia, the engine doesn't need that much torque (power) to spin the flywheel to reach the certain rpm. The result is your car's rpm will increase faster. Additionally, the reduced weight alone will give you a bump in power.

Unorthodox Racing Flywheel

You’ve done a lot of modifications and added a lot of accessories to optimize your vehicle’s performance, but to take it to the next level, you’ve got to do a little bit of surgery, because you’ve just got to have a performance flywheel if you’re going to get all of the acceleration you can out of this beast. When you’ve got a heavy stock flywheel, you’re wasting a lot of your available power just getting the thing moving, and that’s why you’re going to want to get a genuine Unorthodox Racing Flywheel, because this lightweight part starts spinning faster, so you can be transmitting power to your wheels, where it belongs. If you’ve got a turbo vehicle, there’s even more advantage, because the engine needs to rev up before the turbo kicks in, and with a lightweight flywheel, you reach that trigger point much faster. You’ll get exceptional clutch performance, because you’ll get a ferritic nitrocarburized insert for the contact surface, which means that you’ll get a steel insert that hardened using a unique process that makes much more able to handle an aggressive ceramic clutch than an ordinary cast iron or chrome moly wheel. An Unorthodox Racing Flywheel is a thing of beauty and power. Motor enthusiasts always deserve superb customer service when ordering good quality auto parts.
